Hong Kong in 5 Days:
A Photographer’s Route

Five days, one camera, sunsets around 6:55pm (check for your dates).

A route for first-timers who like walking, taking photos and getting out early. For a slow, resort-style trip, pick a few stops from each day.

Day 1: Arrival and The Peak (Wan Chai, The Peak)

  1. Early afternoon — Tourist Octopus card from the Terminal 1 arrivals counter A13 (check current price and counter).
  2. Airport Express into the city: about HKD 115 (check before you go), roughly 24 min.
  3. 15:10 Fat J Char Siu (發達叉燒): Iberico char siu and lard rice. Backup: Soft Thunder Bakery (char siu toast). · Map: Fat J Char Siu · Map: Soft Thunder Bakery
  4. 16:25 Overpass view: shoot down onto the street.
  5. 17:10 Peak Tram, round trip with Sky Terrace 428. Book ahead if you can (check before you go). · Map: Peak Tram · Map: Sky Terrace 428
  6. 17:20 Sky Terrace 428 (凌霄閣摩天台428): the highest viewing platform on the Peak. · Map: Sky Terrace 428
  7. 17:50 Egg tart at Bakehouse, The Peak. · Map: Bakehouse
  8. 18:05 Lugard Road Lookout (盧吉道觀景台): 360-degree harbour view. Sunset is around 6:55pm (check for your dates); stay until about 7:15pm. · Map: Lugard Road Lookout
  9. 19:30 Lan Kwai Fong (蘭桂坊) for nightlife. · Map: Lan Kwai Fong

Day 2: Quarry Bay, Kennedy Town and Sunset (Quarry Bay, Wan Chai, Kennedy Town)

  1. 09:00 Monster Building (Yick Cheong Building, 益昌大廈): there is a queue for the camera spots, and you need to stay quiet. · Map: Monster Building
  2. 10:15 Retro TV art installation.
  3. 11:20 Wan Chai Waterfront. · Map: Wan Chai Waterfront
  4. 12:00 Harbour Station.
  5. 12:50 Chukfo Taipan Roast (竺扶大班燒味): char siu and roast goose rice. · Map: Chukfo Taipan Roast
  6. 14:10 Hung Hing Toys (鴻興玩具): shopping. · Map: Hung Hing Toys
  7. 14:45 Winstons Coffee. · Map: Winstons Coffee
  8. 15:45 Kennedy Town basketball courts. · Map: Kennedy Town basketball courts
  9. 16:25 Kennedy Town new promenade. · Map: Kennedy Town new promenade
  10. 17:05 Near HKU: sun pillar effect, shoot at 17:20 (it depends on the sky; no guarantee).
  11. Bell Swimming Shed (鐘聲泳棚). Arrive by 6:30pm (to confirm). Sunset is around 6:55pm (check for your dates); stay until about 7:15pm. · Map: Bell Swimming Shed
  12. 19:15 Back at the Kennedy Town basketball courts for blue hour. · Map: Kennedy Town basketball courts
  13. 22:00 Mak Man Kee Noodle (麥文記麵家): shrimp wonton noodles. Or Australia Dairy Company (澳洲牛奶公司): scrambled egg toast. · Map: Mak Man Kee Noodle · Map: Australia Dairy Company

Day 3: Lantau and the Harbour (Tung Chung, Ngong Ping, Tsim Sha Tsui)

  1. 08:50 MTR to Tung Chung (about 1 hour).
  2. 10:00 Ngong Ping 360 cable car (昂坪360): going up in the Crystal Plus cabin. Book ahead if you can (check before you go). · Map: Ngong Ping 360
  3. 10:50 Tian Tan Buddha (天壇大佛): 268 steps, free entry (check before you go). Allow about 2.5 hours. · Map: Tian Tan Buddha
  4. 13:20 Ngong Ping Village: vegetarian meal and market snacks.
  5. 14:10 Cable car down in a standard cabin.
  6. 15:40 Rest break.
  7. 16:15 Cheung Hing Kee (祥興記上海生煎包): Shanghai pan-fried buns. · Map: Cheung Hing Kee
  8. 17:10 Cupping Room Coffee. · Map: Cupping Room Coffee
  9. 17:55 Harbour City. Sunset is around 6:55pm (check for your dates); stay until about 7:15pm. · Map: Harbour City
  10. 19:25 Avenue of Stars. · Map: Avenue of Stars
  11. Around 8:20pm: Victoria Harbour night cruise, about 45 min (check departure times).

Day 4: Central and West Kowloon (Central, West Kowloon)

  1. 09:15 Star Ferry from Tsim Sha Tsui to Central.
  2. 09:25 Central Pier No. 9: a good angle on the Observation Wheel. · Map: Central Pier No. 9
  3. 10:00 Central walk: Central Market, Mid-Levels Escalator, Tai Kwun, Fringe Club, Aberdeen Street steps, Central MTR Exit J2. · Map: Central Market · Map: Mid-Levels Escalator · Map: Tai Kwun · Map: Fringe Club · Map: Aberdeen Street
  4. 13:30 Lan Fong Yuen (蘭芳園): birthplace of Hong Kong milk tea. · Map: Lan Fong Yuen
  5. 16:45 Hong Kong Observation Wheel (about 1.5 hours). · Map: Hong Kong Observation Wheel
  6. 18:30 Allow time to reach West Kowloon. Check the ferry route before you go.
  7. 19:00 West Kowloon Cultural District. · Map: West Kowloon Cultural District
  8. 19:30 Inakaya Adin (稻成亞丁): Peking duck, at Elements mall. · Map: Inakaya Adin

Day 5: Kowloon (Yau Ma Tei, Mong Kok, Tsim Sha Tsui)

  1. 10:00 Explore Yau Ma Tei. · Map: Yau Ma Tei
  2. 10:30 London Chinese Restaurant (倫敦大酒樓): trolley dim sum. · Map: London Chinese Restaurant
  3. 11:40 Langham Place: shopping. · Map: Langham Place
  4. 13:00 Optional: Chiikawa shop. It may need a booking (check before you go). · Map: Chiikawa shop
  5. 14:30 Tsim Sha Tsui walk: former KCR Clock Tower, Hong Kong Museum of Art, K11 MUSEA, Signal Hill Garden. · Map: former KCR Clock Tower · Map: Hong Kong Museum of Art · Map: K11 MUSEA · Map: Signal Hill Garden
  6. 17:10 Snacks and bakeries: Hashtag B, Marouf & Puff Bake, Bakehouse (TST). · Map: Bakehouse · Map: Hashtag B · Map: Marouf & Puff Bake
  7. 18:05 ISSUE Hong Kong: souvenirs. · Map: ISSUE Hong Kong
  8. Evening — Airport Express, about HKD 115 (check before you go), roughly 24 min.
